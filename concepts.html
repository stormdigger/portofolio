THREE MOTION STUDIES / ONE STORY

01 / The unfolding room

A physical room breaks into a field of memories. Strongest human connection. Selected as the opening and ending.

02 / Inside the machine

The camera travels through an impossible infrastructure. Strongest sense of scale. Selected as the connective experience.

03 / A question in pieces

Type becomes a living particle sculpture. Strongest graphic impact, but weaker as a complete life story. Used as a single transition.
